Place your speech recording here as speech.wav (uncompressed WAV).

Record 10-20 seconds in a quiet room, preferably at 48000 Hz. Keep a
consistent microphone distance and avoid clipping. Include ordinary speech
and consonants such as s, f, and sh, which help reveal high-frequency loss.
Listen to the recording before using it. A quiet recording is a reference,
not a claim of perfectly noise-free speech.

Suggested text (or use your own language):
"This is my digital signal processing experiment. Speech contains sounds
that change over time. I will add a controlled tone, compare two filters,
and investigate how sampling changes the recorded signal."

Use your own voice, or obtain permission and document the source. Keep the
original recording unchanged. Convert phone M4A recordings to WAV first.
